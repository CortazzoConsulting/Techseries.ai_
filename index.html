One City. Every AI Community. One Day.

Atlanta AI Day is Tech Series’ year-end flagship, a full-day gathering that unites Atlanta’s AI communities under one roof: founders, operators, enterprise and IT leaders, builders, engineers, creatives, investors, and the next wave just getting started.

This is not a traditional conference.

Across keynotes, community-led sessions, live demos, and curated networking, we’ll look back at what actually worked in AI throughout 2026, the year of agents, and look forward at what’s coming next. Not hype. Not theory. Real use cases, real workflows, real operators.

The day is built in blocks, not back-to-back sessions, with real breaks and space to connect, because the best conversations happen in the hallway, not just on stage.

Common questions about attending, speaking, sponsoring, and the TechSeries.ai platform. Don't see your question? Email ethan@techseries.ai.

Attending
Is this event ticketed?+
Yes. Atlanta AI Day tickets are on sale now on GetNetworked, which lists every ticket option. The day includes the keynotes, three panels, featured talks, curated AI networking, speaker office hours and the sponsor demo lounge. Get your ticket ↗
Who should attend TechSeries.ai events?+
Founders, executives, operators, consultants, and technology leaders who are actively thinking about AI adoption — whether you're just getting started or already deploying at scale. Our April 30 event drew 49% founders and 18% C-level executives. If you have a real decision to make about AI, this room is built for you.
What actually happens at the event?+
Each TECH SERIES evening runs on the same shape: registration and AI-curated networking, a keynote or fireside chat, a panel, then a networking reception. Atlanta AI Day on December 10 runs 8:15 AM to 4:30 PM: an opening keynote, three panels and featured talks in the morning and early afternoon, then AI networking, speaker office hours and a sponsor demo lounge until 4:30 PM.
Where are events held?+
All 2026 monthly events are held at Life Time Work Sandy Springs — 5600 Roswell Rd Building C, Sandy Springs, GA 30342. It's one of Atlanta's most premium collaborative workspaces with 100+ companies based on-site, on-site parking, and a professional event setup. Atlanta AI Day is at the same venue on December 10.
How do I register?+
All registrations are handled through GetNetworked. Click any 'Register' button on this site and you'll be taken directly to the event page where you can RSVP, pay, and connect with other attendees before the event. You can also visit app.getnetworked.com/techseries.ai to see all upcoming events.
What should I expect from the networking?+
At our April 30 kickoff, 92% of pre-registered attendees said they were open to introductions. The GetNetworked platform gives every attendee a private event chat to connect before, during, and after the event, and AI-powered matches so you know who to meet. The room is set up for curated conversations, not a crowded mixer.
Is this only for Atlanta-based people?+
The events are held in Atlanta at Life Time Work Sandy Springs, but we regularly have attendees from across the Southeast and beyond. If you're in Atlanta or visiting, these events are worth building your schedule around.
Speaking
How can I speak at a TechSeries.ai event?+
We feature practitioners with real AI implementation results — not keynote-circuit speakers. If your organization has deployed AI systems, driven measurable ROI, or built AI-powered workflows that others can learn from, we want to hear from you. Submit a speaking inquiry through the Sponsors page and we'll be in touch within 48 hours.
What kind of speakers do you feature?+
Every speaker at TechSeries.ai has real implementation experience. We've featured executives from Southwest Airlines, Credo AI, Spark Novus, and MotorDNA — alongside founders, operators, and builders who have shipped AI products in-market. No theorists. No slide-deck-only presentations. Real operators who have done the work.

Most AI events are either giant conferences (expensive, impersonal) or casual meetups (low quality). Every sponsor is reviewed for relevance. No fluff. All execution.

TechSeries.ai is the flagship event series from GetNetworked — Atlanta's community platform for AI business leaders. We produce four curated evenings and a full-day flagship each year at Life Time Work in Sandy Springs, bringing together the region's top operators, founders, and executives.

Our events aren't panels about AI's potential. They're rooms full of people actively implementing it — sharing real numbers, real failures, and real frameworks. Every session follows a structured program: keynote, executive panel, builder spotlight, and meaningful networking.

The 2026 series follows a deliberate arc: from understanding what AI-native companies actually look like, through the economics of scaling AI and building AI-ready teams, to an industry deep-dive on AI in real estate — closing with Atlanta AI Day: one city, every AI community, one day.
