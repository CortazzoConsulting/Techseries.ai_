Attending
Is this event ticketed?+
Events are ticketed. For October 22 (AI in Real Estate): General Admission $30, VIP Connect $49 (limited), and a Team Pass for 4 attendees at $75. Tickets include the full program, curated networking, AI-powered attendee matches, and the networking reception. Atlanta AI Day on December 10 is ticketed separately.
Who should attend TechSeries.ai events?+
Founders, executives, operators, consultants, and technology leaders who are actively thinking about AI adoption — whether you're just getting started or already deploying at scale. Our April 30 event drew 49% founders and 18% C-level executives. If you have a real decision to make about AI, this room is built for you.
What actually happens at the event?+
Doors open at 2:00 PM for complimentary coworking. At 4:00 PM the program kicks off with registration and a vendor showcase. From 4:45–5:15 PM there's a keynote session, followed by fireside chats and breakout conversations until 5:45 PM. The evening closes with a networking reception and vendor engagement until 7:30 PM.
Where are events held?+
All 2026 monthly events are held at Life Time Work Sandy Springs — 5600 Roswell Rd Building C, Sandy Springs, GA 30342. It's one of Atlanta's most premium collaborative workspaces with 100+ companies based on-site, on-site parking, and a professional event setup. Atlanta AI Day is at the same venue on December 10.
How do I register?+
All registrations are handled through Getnetworked. Click any 'Register' button on this site and you'll be taken directly to the event page where you can RSVP, pay, and connect with other attendees before the event. You can also visit app.getnetworked.com/techseries.ai to see all upcoming events.
What should I expect from the networking?+
Our events average 92% of attendees open to introductions. The Getnetworked platform gives every attendee a private event chat to connect before, during, and after the event. The venue layout is specifically designed for curated conversations — not a crowded mixer. Expect meaningful connections with real decision-makers.
Is this only for Atlanta-based people?+
The events are held in Atlanta at Life Time Work Sandy Springs, but we regularly have attendees from across the Southeast and beyond. If you're in Atlanta or visiting, these events are worth building your schedule around.

FAQ

Common Questions

How much do events cost?+
For October 22 (AI in Real Estate): General Admission $30, VIP Connect $49, and a Team Pass for 4 at $75. Tickets include the full program, curated networking, AI-powered attendee matches, and the networking reception. Atlanta AI Day on December 10 is ticketed separately.
Who should attend TechSeries.ai events?+
Founders, executives, operators, consultants, and technology leaders who are actively thinking about AI adoption — whether you're just getting started or already deploying at scale. If you have a real decision to make about AI, this room is for you.
Where are events held?+
All 2026 events are held at Life Time Work Sandy Springs — 5600 Roswell Rd Building C, Sandy Springs, GA 30342. It's one of Atlanta's most premium collaborative workspaces with parking on site.
How can I speak at a TechSeries.ai event?+
We feature practitioners with real AI implementation results — not keynote-circuit speakers. If your organization has deployed AI systems, driven measurable ROI, or built AI-powered workflows, submit a speaking inquiry through our Sponsors page and we'll be in touch.
How can my company sponsor an event?+
Single-event sponsorships run Platinum ($2,000), Gold ($1,000), Silver ($500), and Bronze ($250), plus a Vendor Booth ($500). Series, Atlanta AI Day, and Annual Partnership packages bundle multiple events at up to 23% savings. Every tier includes booth or showcase space, logo placement, and tickets. Visit our Sponsors page to submit an inquiry.
Is TechSeries.ai affiliated with Getnetworked?+
Yes — TechSeries.ai is the flagship event series from Getnetworked, Atlanta's AI business community platform. All registrations and event management run through the Getnetworked platform at app.getnetworked.com.
What is Atlanta AI Day in December?+
Atlanta AI Day is the year-end flagship — a full day on December 10, 2026 at Life Time Work Sandy Springs uniting Atlanta’s AI communities for keynotes, community-led sessions, live demos, and curated networking.
